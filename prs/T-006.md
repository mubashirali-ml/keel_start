## Summary

Implements the Law Agent specialist. Each turn spots legal issues deterministically and
stores them in `state.legal_issues`, classifies the founder's message (`law.intent`), and
either answers the question (`law.answer`, with the relevant spotted issues listed under the
answer) or handles a draft request: with locked facts it returns `DraftDocuments` +
`OpenView("legal")` and hands back; without, it explains the Manager must confirm the facts
first and hands back. Every Law message ends with `LEGAL_NOTICE`; `LLMOutputError` (and an
empty model answer) becomes a friendly apology. `draft_documents` fills Markdown templates
(IP assignment, Bylaws / Operating Agreement Summary, one 83(b) per founder) and quotes
share/unit counts only from `cap_table.rows[i].shares`. Deterministic fake handlers for
`law.intent` and `law.answer` make the demo read naturally from the case facts.

## Notes for reviewers

- The ADR-0013 vesting defaults are re-stated in `issues.resolved_vesting` because
  `rules.validation` is T-002's (still a stub on `main`). Behaviour is the same rule.
- `draft_documents` raises `FactsNotReadyError` when the cap table's entity type or founder
  ids do not match the facts (a stale cap table), rather than drafting wrong documents.
- Every Law message (answers, draft replies, apologies) ends with `LEGAL_NOTICE`, not only
  answers. The Law Agent never raises for LLM failures; it does not catch `FakeLLMMissingHandler`
  (tests must fail loudly, ADR-0010).
