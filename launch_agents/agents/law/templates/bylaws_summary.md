## Board of directors and officers

The founders listed above are the initial directors. The board manages the Company's business, appoints the officers (a Chief Executive Officer, a Secretary and a Treasurer, who may be the same person) and approves any new stock issuance. The board acts by majority vote at a meeting or by unanimous written consent.

- Chief Executive Officer: [CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER]
- Secretary: [SECRETARY]

## Stockholders

Stockholders elect the directors at an annual meeting. Each share carries one vote. A majority of the outstanding stock, present in person or by proxy, is a quorum. Stockholders may also act by written consent.

## Transfers of stock

A founder who wants to sell or give away stock must first offer it to the Company (a right of first refusal), except for transfers to a family trust for estate planning.

## Changing the bylaws

The board or the stockholders may change the bylaws by majority vote.
